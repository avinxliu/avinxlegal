FlowFlight Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Developer: Jinming Liu
Contact: mingx.app@outlook.com

FlowFlight is a flight-themed focus app. This policy explains how the app handles your data. Your data stays on your device. When you are signed in to iCloud, it syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud database. We do not run our own server for this content, and we do not send it to advertising or analytics services.

Data the app stores

  • Habits: name, icon, color, target duration, reminder times, and order. Archived habits remain stored.
  • Focus sessions: start and end times, planned duration, actual duration, and whether the session was completed or stopped.
  • Flight logs: flight details generated by the app, plus departure and arrival airport codes. Airport names and map coordinates come from the catalog bundled with the app. The app does not read your location.
  • Notes: text you write, and any photo you attach to that note.
  • Flight log cards: the color theme for that day, and which note supplies the cover photo.
  • Profile: display name and avatar. Rank is calculated from your cumulative successful focus minutes when shown. It is not stored as a separate record.
  • Preferences: how airport codes are shown, which day starts the week, and the flight distance unit. These sync with iCloud. Light, dark, or system appearance is stored only on that device.
  • Purchase status: the Premium entitlement read from the App Store, so purchased features can stay unlocked.

Notes and sync

Note text is stored in plain text in the app database on the device. When it syncs to iCloud, Apple’s system encryption protects that text. Photos attached to notes sync with iCloud.

Without an iCloud account, you can still read and edit notes and add photos on the device. Those changes are not synced to other devices.

Habit reminders require notification permission. Focus-completion alerts and Live Activities follow the system. Settings does not include separate master switches for those two.

Photos

When you choose an avatar or a note photo, you hand that image over through the system photo picker. The app uses only the image you select, for your avatar or a flight log card. Saving or sharing a flight log card is handled by the system share or save sheet.

Maps and motion

The map shows the focus route. The app does not ask for location permission. The home map may tilt slightly with the device’s motion. That motion is used only for the picture on screen. It is not saved or uploaded.

Widgets and Live Activities

Widgets and Live Activities read a local, read-only snapshot of the current focus session through an App Group, such as the habit name, end time, and today’s focus time. That snapshot stays on your device and is used to draw the system surfaces.

Payments

Purchases, subscriptions, and restore are handled by Apple. We do not receive your card number or payment password.

Tracking and third parties

The app does not use an advertising identifier. It does not include ads, third-party analytics, or a third-party crash reporter. We do not sell your data, and we do not provide notes, photos, or focus records to third parties for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device and in iCloud until you delete it. Delete All Data in Settings removes habits, focus records, flight logs, notes, themes, your avatar, and reminders from this device and from iCloud. An existing subscription is not cancelled. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on that device. You can also manage the iCloud copy in system settings.

This version does not export your data to a file.

Children

FlowFlight is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, email the address above.

Changes

We will update this page when features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page is the one that applies.
